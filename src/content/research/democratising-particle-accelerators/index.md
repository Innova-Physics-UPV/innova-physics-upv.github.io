Few universities let undergraduates near an accelerator. This poster makes the case that building one is the best way to learn how one works, and shows how a student team at the Universitat Politècnica de València has started.

## Motivation

Accelerator physics is taught late, if at all, before graduate school. Students meet the field through lectures and simulations, rarely through hardware.

## The team

Students from different degrees, organised in four departments: applied physics, electronics and control, technical design, and communication.

## Model-0

A glow-discharge demonstrator: a glass tube, a vacuum pump and a high-voltage supply. At about 1&nbsp;Torr (built) the gas glows along the path of the electrons.

## ALOHA

A linear open-hardware accelerator, built in stages: the electron source first (simulated), then bunching and acceleration to a design target of 1&nbsp;MeV.

## Education

Every stage is documented in the open, so other teams can build their own.
